该吃药啦隐私政策

更新及生效日期：2026 年 11 月 22 日

运营者：yolo 工作室

联系邮箱：syx6552627@sina.com

1. 本机处理

该吃药啦是一款单机服药提醒工具。您可以不注册账号直接使用。您录入的药品名称、适应症、用法用量、库存、有效期、服药计划、服药记录以及您选取的药品照片可能包含健康信息。应用将这些资料保存在本机私有存储，用于生成提醒、展示历史、扣减库存和识别药盒。应用安装包未申请互联网权限，开发者不会通过应用接收、上传、出售或共享上述资料。

2. 权限和系统功能

相机权限仅在您主动拍摄药盒时使用。选取照片时，应用只读取您通过系统照片选择器指定的文件。拒绝相机权限后仍可手动录入。通知、精确闹钟、全屏提醒和后台媒体播放用于按计划发出通知和播放语音。开机广播用于恢复已设提醒，唤醒锁用于完成通知投递和播放。关闭这些权限会影响相应提醒，您可以随时在系统设置中调整。锁屏上能否看到药名和用量由系统的锁屏通知设置决定。

3. 第三方组件

Google LLC 提供的 Google ML Kit Text Recognition（中文，本地模型）只在您选择识别照片时处理所选图片，识别输入和结果在本机处理。Google 的通用 SDK 说明列有诊断和使用指标，本应用未授予 SDK 直接联网的权限。详见 https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms 和 https://policies.google.com/privacy 。应用还使用 AndroidX、Jetpack Compose、Room、DataStore、WorkManager、Hilt 和 Coil，用于界面、本地数据存储、后台调度和本地图片加载。

4. 备份、保存期限和删除

本机资料保存到您将其删除或卸载应用为止。您可在药箱、计划和记录界面查看、更正资料，在「设置 → 本机数据」导出备份 ZIP，或在空数据的新安装中导入备份。备份含健康资料且未加密，由您决定保存位置。选择云盘时，目标服务商会按其自身规则处理该文件。应用内「删除本机数据」会清除药品、照片、计划、库存和记录，并撤销相应提醒。已导出到应用外的备份由您自行删除。

5. 联系与权利

您可通过上述邮箱请求协助查阅、复制、更正或删除个人信息，也可提出投诉。我们会在收到请求后 15 个工作日内处理。您主动提供的邮箱、反馈内容及您随信附带的资料仅用于答复和处理请求。请尽量不要在反馈中附上用药记录或照片。政策变更会通过本页和应用内隐私政策更新。

Meds On Time Privacy Policy

Updated and effective 22 November 2026

Publisher: yolo 工作室

Contact: syx6552627@sina.com

1. On-device processing

Meds On Time is an offline medication reminder. You can use it without an account. Medicine names, indications, directions, quantities, expiry dates, schedules, dose history, and medicine photos you add may contain health information. The app stores them in private storage on your device to show records, schedule alarms, track stock, and read package labels. The app has no internet permission. The publisher does not receive, upload, sell, or share this data through the app.

2. Permissions and system features

The app asks for the camera when you choose to take a package photo. The system picker gives the app access only to files you select. You can enter details manually if you decline camera access. Notifications, exact alarms, full-screen alarms, and foreground audio playback are used for scheduled alerts. The app restores reminders after a restart and briefly keeps the device awake to deliver an alert or play audio. You can change permissions in system settings. Whether medicine details appear on your lock screen depends on your lock-screen notification settings.

3. Third-party components

Google LLC's Google ML Kit Text Recognition with a bundled Chinese model processes the photos you choose to read. Photo input and recognition results are processed on the device. Google's general SDK terms describe diagnostics and usage metrics. This app does not grant the SDK direct internet access. See https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms and https://policies.google.com/privacy . The app also uses AndroidX, Jetpack Compose, Room, DataStore, WorkManager, Hilt, and Coil for its interface, local storage, background scheduling, and local photo display.

4. Backups, retention, and deletion

Local records are retained until you delete them or uninstall the app. You can view and correct entries, export them from Settings → On-device data, and restore a backup into an empty installation. Backups are unencrypted ZIP files containing health information. You select the destination. If you select a cloud storage provider, it handles the backup under its own terms. Delete on-device data clears medicines, photos, plans, stock, and records, and cancels their alarms. Exported copies remain at the locations you selected and can be deleted there.

5. Requests and contact

Use the email above to request help accessing, copying, correcting, or deleting personal information, or to raise a complaint. We will handle requests within 15 business days. Your email address and any information you send with a request are used to respond to it. Avoid attaching medicine records or photos unless needed to explain the issue. Any changes to this policy will be reflected here and in the app.
